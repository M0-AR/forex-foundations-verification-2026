🌱 Beginner guide — read this and you are a professional student

You will know more than most interview candidates after these 10 ideas. Each is one sentence, then one example with the real verified numbers from this repo.

1 · Trading
Buy low, sell high
Buy €1 for $1.10, sell at $1.12 → keep $0.02. Same for gold, stocks, houses.
2 · Forex
$9.6tn / day
Official April-2025 turnover, +28% vs 2022. Open 24h Mon–Fri, closed Sat/Sun.
3 · Sessions
12–17 UTC wins
Overlap averaged 12.62 pips/hr vs Asia 6.58 (1.92×). Peak hours: 12, 13, 14 UTC.
4 · Candles
O · H · L · C
Body = open→close, wicks = high/low. Green = up. Weekly = exact sum of dailies (1301→262).
5 · Orders
Market + 4 pendings
Buy/sell now; buy-limit/sell-limit (bounce) vs buy-stop/sell-stop (continue). Entry + TP + SL.
6 · Pips & lots
$0.10 · $1 · $10
Per pip on EURUSD: 0.01 lot→$0.10, 0.10→$1.00, 1.00→$10.00. Verified @1.12574577.
7 · Broker costs
Spread + fee
641-trade test: gross −$1,890 → net −$3,300. Costs took $1,410. Model them or fail.
8 · Leverage
Margin lock
1.0 lot @100× locks $1,125.75 margin for $100k control. 100 pips wrong = −$1,000.
9 · Risk math
30% can win
0.30×3R − 0.70×1R = +0.20R/trade → +2R per 10 trades. Expectancy beats win-rate.
10 · News
NFP = wide days
First Fridays averaged 85.79 vs 67.51 pips (1.27×, p=0.007). Volatility, not direction.

How it works (the no-peek engine, plain words)

close[t] decides → open[t+1] fills (never today's close)
same bar hits TP+SL → counts as SL (pessimistic)
net = gross − spread·value − commission − swap
one trade at a time · 48-bar timeout · 5,000 order-shuffles for luck-check

FAQ

Do I need money or an account? No. Everything runs on free public data. Use a demo account if you practice orders.
Which session should a beginner watch first? 12:00–17:00 UTC. It moved 1.92× more than Asia in our sample.
Why did the example strategy lose? It was intentionally naive (5-day momentum, tight 30/10). It proves costs ($1,410 on 641 trades) and the need for a real edge + walk-forward validation.
Is leverage free money? No. It locks margin ($1,125.75 per 1.0 lot @100× here) and multiplies losses equally (100 pips = −$1,000). Overnight swap is separate — demand swap-free terms in writing if relevant.
Can I trust one backtest? No. Require walk-forward out-of-sample, cross-pair generality (≥3/5 pairs), spread shocks, and 30–50 forward trades before judging.
How do I make my own strategy? Replace signals in 09_full_benchmark.py, keep the engine, run make verify.

Risks & honesty

Educational research only — not financial advice. Leveraged FX can lose rapidly and (depending on jurisdiction/account) more than the deposit. Most beginners lose by over-sizing. Never risk needed money. Demo first (2–4 weeks) → micro-live (4–8 weeks) → scale only if live stays within 1σ of tested expectations. Past (and backtested) performance does not predict the future.
